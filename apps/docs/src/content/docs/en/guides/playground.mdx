The Playground is a safe interactive space to explore updates, snapshots, and patches.

## Quick Tour

1. Change `count` and `user.name`.
2. Toggle batching and compare patch output.
3. Compare leaf vs root subscriptions.

## Suggested Experiments

- Modify deep fields and inspect patch paths.
- Compare `snapshot()` output to `get()`.
- Switch schedules and observe notification frequency.
