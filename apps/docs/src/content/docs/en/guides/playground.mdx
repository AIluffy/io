This page provides a safe, interactive playground to explore IO behavior without executing arbitrary code.

## 30-second recipe

1. Open the Playground.
2. Update `count` and `user.name` and watch the UI update.
3. Repeat the same changes inside and outside batching and compare the patch output.

## What to try

- Increment counters with and without batching.
- Toggle nested values and observe patch output in DevTools.
- Compare snapshots before and after updates.
- Subscribe to a leaf vs subscribe at the root and compare what you receive.
